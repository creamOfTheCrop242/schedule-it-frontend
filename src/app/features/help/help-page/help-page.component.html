Help & install

The Guide Log helps you track logs, tasks, goals, and habits in one place.

Using the app

  • Dashboard shows a snapshot of upcoming tasks and recent activity.
  • Logs are journal-style entries you can add, view, and edit from the Logs tab.
  • Tasks are to-dos with their own list and detail views.
  • Goals & habits share the middle tab: open the menu to go to Goals or Habits, then add or track what matters to you.
  • Settings (gear in the header) is where you update your name or sign out.

Install as an app

Installing adds an icon to your home screen and often opens the app in its own window, like a native app.

  • Chrome / Edge (desktop or Android): look for “Install app” or “Install The Guide Log” in the browser menu, or use the prompt if the site offers one.
  • Safari (iPhone / iPad): tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.
  • Safari (Mac): File → Add to Dock (wording may vary by macOS version).

On some browsers you may see an install banner at the bottom of the screen; you can use that or the steps above.
