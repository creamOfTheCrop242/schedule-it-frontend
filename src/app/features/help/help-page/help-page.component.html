Help & install

The Guide Log is a life log: journals (logs), tasks, habits, numeric log progress goals, personal goals tied to your activity, optional AI summaries and coaching, and a shared daily Story of the day.

Home

  • Dashboard shows quick links to habits, log progress, personal goals, and Story of the day (SOTD), plus previews of habits, open tasks, and recent logs.

Logs

  • Logs are journal-style entries—add, filter, and open each from Logs.
  • When adding or editing a log you can attach an optional personal goal so that log counts toward that goal’s linked list and AI coaching.
  • AI summary on the logs screen sends excerpts from logs that match your current filters (up to 50) to the model for a blunt read on patterns—not medical advice.

Tasks

  • Tasks live under Tasks; you can optionally link a task to a personal goal for your own organization.

Log progress goals

  • Open the middle tab (scoreboard icon) → Log goals or go to Log progress goal.
  • Set a daily target and choose whether you track logs added or logs completed. Weekly, monthly, and yearly bars use the same daily number (×7 / ×30 / ×365).

Personal goals

  • Personal goals are named aims—not the same as log progress goals. Create them from the same middle-tab menu, then attach them on log or task forms.
  • A goal’s detail page lists linked logs and offers AI coaching using up to fifty of those logs (notes included when present). Coaching does not read tasks; it needs logs assigned to the goal.

Habits

  • From the middle tab choose Habits: each habit has a cadence and logs per period—the minimum log check-ins per period that count that period toward your streak.

Story of the day

  • One shared AI-written motivational piece per UTC calendar day (everyone sees the same story until the next UTC midnight). Each day’s prompt includes a rotating world region so settings vary.
  • Reach it from the dashboard quick action SOTD or Story of the day.

Navigation

  • Bottom bar: Home, Logs, Add (log or task), Tasks, and Goals & habits (opens Log goals, Personal goals, or Habits).
  • Settings (gear in the header) is for your name and signing out.

Install as an app

Installing adds an icon to your home screen and often opens the app in its own window, like a native app.

  • Chrome / Edge (desktop or Android): look for “Install app” or “Install The Guide Log” in the browser menu, or use the prompt if the site offers one.
  • Safari (iPhone / iPad): tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.
  • Safari (Mac): File → Add to Dock (wording may vary by macOS version).

On some browsers you may see an install banner at the bottom of the screen; you can use that or the steps above.
